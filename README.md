# 🕯️ Ghost in the Haveli (हवेली की भटकती रूह)
### *A Haunted Haveli Voice Horror Experience Powered by Sarvam AI*

---

## 🏰 About the Experience

Step into a 300-year-old abandoned Rajasthani Haveli shrouded in total darkness. As you hold a single flickering brass candle, you encounter the trapped royal spirit of **Rani Roopmati** (रानी रूपमती). 

The ghost speaks **strictly in a regional dialect** (Hindi, Bengali, Marathi) and tests your courage with cryptic riddles. You interact with her using your **real voice**. Her mood dynamically shifts based on your tone, respect, and wisdom:

- **Appease her** with humility and correct riddle answers to calm the atmosphere, steady the candle flame, and earn her guidance.
- **Enrage her** with disrespect, mockery, or arrogance to turn the candlelight blood-red, rattle ancient ghungroos (ankle bells), and unleash terrifying jumpscares!

---

## ⚡ Key Features

1. **Regional Ghost Voice (Sarvam AI Bulbul:v3)**:
   - Authentic Indian voice synthesis with dynamic pitch & pace modulation matching the ghost's anger.
   - Choose between **Hindi (Rajasthan Haveli)**, **Bengali (Bengal Rajbari)**, or **Marathi (Peshwa Wada)**.
   - Optional archaic English subtitles toggle for non-native speakers.

2. **Voice Interaction (Speech-to-Text)**:
   - Speak directly to the ghost using Web Speech Recognition (click or hold `Spacebar`).
   - Mystic pulsing audio-reactive aura around the microphone.
   - Emergency keyboard input drawer for quiet environments.

3. **Dynamic Candlelight & Dark Haveli Visuals (Canvas 2D Engine)**:
   - True atmospheric darkness cut only by your candle's radial glow.
   - Realistic flame physics with velocity wobble, blue core, smoke tendrils, and floating embers.
   - Flame reacts in real-time to ghost anger: calm glow ➔ frantic fluttering ➔ violent sputtering ➔ blackout!
   - Sandstone Mehraab arches, carved jharokha balconies with lightning outside, an antique portrait whose eyes track your candle, and a cursed broken mirror.

4. **Procedural Haunted Soundscape (Web Audio API)**:
   - High-fidelity creaky wooden door opening sound.
   - Resonant binaural wind howling through empty stone corridors.
   - Distant metallic ghungroo (dancer ankle bells) chimes.
   - Visceral pounding heartbeat that speeds up as anger crosses 50%.

5. **The 3 Cursed Secrets of the Haveli**:
   - Secret 1: *The Shattered Mirror (टूटा दर्पण)*
   - Secret 2: *The Silent Ghungroos (मौन घुंघरू)*
   - Secret 3: *The Trapped Soul's Salvation (मुक्ति का मार्ग)*

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Open your terminal in this directory:
   ```bash
   cd C:\Users\ASUS\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\ghost-in-the-haveli
   ```

2. Start the local server:
   ```bash
   python app.py
   ```

3. Open your browser at:
   ```
   http://127.0.0.1:5000
   ```

4. Click **"कपाट खोलें (Enter Haveli)"** to hear the creaking gates open and begin your encounter!
